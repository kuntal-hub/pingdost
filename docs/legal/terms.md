## 1. What PingDost is
PingDost is an end-to-end encrypted messenger for Android and the web, with optional Bluetooth nearby messaging and relaying, and one-to-one calls. It is free to use and its source code is published under the GNU AGPL-3.0-or-later.

## 2. Your account
- You need a username and a verified email address (or Google sign-in). Keep your password and recovery phrase private.
- Your phone is your account's primary device. You can link browsers by scanning a QR code with the phone.
- **Your history lives on your devices.** If you lose every device and have no backup, your messages are gone. We cannot recover them.

## 3. Acceptable use
Do not use PingDost to:
- break the law, or harm, harass, threaten or exploit anyone, including children;
- send spam or bulk unsolicited messages, or create accounts automatically;
- attack, overload or probe the service, or other users' devices (including over Bluetooth relaying);
- get around limits (rate limits, file sizes, quotas) or other people's blocks.
Because messages are end-to-end encrypted, we cannot see them. We act on reports users send (with the messages they choose to attach) and on abuse we can see in metadata (for example, mass sending). We may limit, suspend or close accounts that break these terms.

## 4. Service limits
- Encrypted messages and files for offline recipients wait on our server for at most **14 days**, then are deleted, delivered or not.
- Files are limited to **100 MB**; images and videos may be compressed. Nearby (no-Internet) file transfers are slower and limited (25 MB by default).
- Nearby messaging and relaying are best effort: phones may stop them in the background, and delivery over the mesh is not guaranteed.
- Calls are one-to-one; group calls are not offered.
